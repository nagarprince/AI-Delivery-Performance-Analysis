AI Delivery Performance — Key Analyst Insights

1.Delivery performance is below the target
The delay rate is 21.36%, which is higher than the 20% target.
This shows that delivery performance needs improvement.

2.Weather has a clear impact on delays
Delay rates increase from around 2% in cold weather to 5.46% during storms.
This suggests that weather conditions should be considered when planning delivery times.

3.Express delivery has the highest delay time
Express deliveries have an average delay of 1.96 hours, much higher than the other delivery modes.
Faster delivery promises may be creating more pressure on the delivery process.

4.Delays are spread across all regions
Central, West, North, and South have similar delayed deliveries, while East is slightly lower.
This suggests the problem is not limited to one region and may be related to the overall delivery process.

5.Most delayed deliveries are still manageable
Around 78% of deliveries are early, while about 21% are delayed.
Most delays are in the slight-delay category, so improving these smaller delays could have a significant effect on the overall delay rate.
